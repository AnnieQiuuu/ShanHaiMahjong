# 山海麻将 art direction

- Approved tile base: `assets/tile/handpainted_tile_v2.png`.
- Original pet portraits `assets/pets/no_XXX_icon.png` must not be redrawn or distorted.
- Thick irregular brown hand-painted contour, flat bright color shapes, warm cream face, organic orange border and jade-green side.
- Avoid metallic gradients, glossy ceramics, photorealistic bevels and glass UI.
- Maintain readability at real game size in a crowded multilayer stack.
- Review both single-tile close-up and a whole-stack view before approving art changes.
